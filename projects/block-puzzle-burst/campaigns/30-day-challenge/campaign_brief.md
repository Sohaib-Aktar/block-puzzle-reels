# 30-Day Challenge Campaign Brief
## Block Puzzle Burst — Instagram Launch Campaign

**Project:** Block Puzzle Burst  
**Campaign ID:** 30-day-challenge  
**Platform:** Instagram Reels (cross-post to Facebook Reels, repurpose to YouTube Shorts)  
**Duration:** 30 consecutive days  
**Primary KPI:** Comments → Reel watch time / retention → Shares → Profile visits → Link-in-bio clicks

## Objective
Build an audience from zero, prove organic growth, and generate measurable Play Store installs traced to social. Target: 150–500 followers and 30–100 attributed installs by Day 30.

## Content strategy
- One Reel per day, 12–25 seconds
- No paid promotion in Phase 1 (organic only)
- Two-part content loops: Challenge Reel (no answer) → Answer Reel next day
- English on-screen text. BN/HI in post captions only.
- Post time: 7:00–8:30 PM IST (1:30–3:00 PM UK time)
- Reply to every comment in the first hour

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Days | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Satisfying clears | 1,2,5,7,8,14,16,17,22,23,24 | 8x combo, slow-mo burst |
| Puzzle challenges | 3,10,13,18,25,27 | A/B/C solve-it |
| Founder dev log | 1,6,11,15,20,21,28,29 | real download numbers |
| Culture & community | 4,12,19,26,30 | chai break, commute POV |

## Weekly rhythm (post-Day 30 maintenance)
Mon: Satisfying clear · Tue: Tips · Wed: Puzzle challenge · Thu: Dev log  
Fri: Clear/trick shot · Sat: Culture/community · Sun: Score challenge

## Hashtag strategy (3–5 per post)
One broad + two niche + one local. Rotate, do not repeat exact same set every day.  
Pool: #blockpuzzle #puzzlegame #offlinegames #indiegamedev #mobilegaming #brainteaser #puzzlechallenge #bengalimemes #casuagaming #gamedevelopment #solodev

## CTA rotation
Every Reel ends with ONE clear ask. Rotate: comment / save / share score card / tap link in bio.

## Two-part challenge loop
- Reel N: Challenge (A/B/C, no answer shown). CTA: "Comment A, B or C. Answer in next Reel."
- Reel N+1 or Story: Reveal answer. CTA: "Were you right? Play the game — link in bio."

## Deliverables per content piece
Each `outputs/block-puzzle-burst/30-day-challenge/<content-id>/` folder must contain:
- `brief.md` — this day's specific brief
- `captions/instagram.txt` — ready-to-paste caption (EN primary, BN+HI below)
- `final/instagram_reel.mp4` — approved MP4, 1080×1920
- `qc_report.md` — render metadata, verified duration, no-BN-on-screen check

## Rules (non-negotiable)
1. English on-screen text only. BN/HI in captions, never burned into video.
2. Use only authentic gameplay from recorded screen captures.
3. Never fabricate download numbers, reviews, or gameplay.
4. Never auto-publish. All files are for manual review first.
5. Never overwrite an approved final file. Use revision suffix `_v2`, `_v3`.
6. Blur Play Console sensitive data before any screen recording appears on camera.
