# Verified FAQs — Block Puzzle Burst

**Q1: Is Block Puzzle Burst completely free?**  
A: Yes! The game is 100% free to download and play from Google Play.

**Q2: Does it require an internet connection or WiFi?**  
A: No! You can play completely offline in Airplane Mode. It is designed specifically for travel, flights, subways, and low-signal situations.

**Q3: Are there ads?**  
A: Yes, ads are included to keep the game completely free for everyone. Ads do not interrupt active moves.

**Q4: Is there a time limit or countdown timer?**  
A: No! Unlike action puzzle games, there is no timer. You can think as long as you want before placing each block.

**Q5: Who made this game?**  
A: A solo indie developer from West Bengal, India, creating relaxing, offline-first mobile games under Pyapiras Games.

**Q6: How does scoring and combos work?**  
A: Clearing full rows or columns gives points. When you clear lines on consecutive moves, your combo multiplier stacks up (2X, 4X, 6X, 8X+) for huge score boosts!
