# Brand Guidelines — Pyapiras Games (Block Puzzle Burst)

---

## 1. Brand Identity
- **Studio Name:** Pyapiras Games
- **Game Title:** Block Puzzle Burst
- **Handle:** `@PyapirasGames` / `@BlockPuzzleBurst`
- **Tone of Voice:** Friendly, humble, clever, encouraging, relaxing, authentic indie developer journey.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Purple:** `#7B2FBE` (Canvas & primary UI background glow)
- **Deep Purple:** `#4A148C` (Vignette & contrast backing)
- **Gold Highlight:** `#FFD700` (High scores, combo multipliers, CTAs)
- **Soft Gold Glow:** `rgba(255, 215, 0, 0.4)`
- **Canvas Dark Base:** `#0D0B18` (High contrast AMOLED dark background)
- **Piece Colors:**
  - Cyan (L-Piece): `#00BFFF`
  - Yellow (Bar Piece): `#FFD700`
  - Purple (Single Block): `#B44FE8`

---

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Badges:** `Montserrat`, `Impact`, `Arial Black`, bold sans-serif.
- **Body & Captions:** `Inter`, `system-ui`, clean readable grotesque sans.
- **Number Counters:** Tabular numbers for countdowns and scores (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

---

## 4. On-Screen Safe Zones
- **Top Safe Area:** Inset 192px (clear of Instagram search bar and account pill).
- **Bottom Safe Area:** Inset 280px (clear of caption, audio marquee, and action icons).
- **Right Edge:** Inset 120px (clear of Instagram like/comment/share icons).
- **Central Action Zone:** All badges, puzzle trays, and CTAs must sit in the central 900×1400px safe viewport.
