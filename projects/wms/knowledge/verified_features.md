## 1. Verified Core Capabilities
- **Inward Goods Receipt (GRN):** Barcode scan on receipt, batch/lot tracking, expiry logging.
- **Bin & Aisle Location:** Dynamic bin assignment (aisle, rack, shelf) to optimize warehouse storage density.
- **Wave & Zone Picking:** Barcode-guided pick lists with FIFO/FEFO picking rules to eliminate pick errors.
- **Packing & Dispatch:** Shipping label printing, package weight verification, courier handover manifest.
- **Cycle Counting & Audit:** Stock reconciliation without shutting down warehouse operations.
