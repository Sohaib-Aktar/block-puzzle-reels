# Verified Features — Expand eziSales

**Product:** Expand eziSales (Field Sales Automation / SFA)  
**Verification Level:** CORE WORKFLOWS VERIFIED (Details pending user confirmation)

---

## 1. Verified Core Capabilities
- **Digital Order Booking:** Field sales executives book retailer orders on mobile without paper slips.
- **Beat & Route Management:** Daily scheduled customer visits and GPS-stamped check-ins.
- **Live Stock & Schemes:** Real-time visibility of SKU pricing, tax, and promotional discounts.
- **Payment Collection Logging:** Captures outstanding ledger balances, payment receipts, and collection notes.
- **Offline Resiliency:** Orders recorded without active internet sync automatically upon reconnection.
- **Central ERP Synchronization:** Direct cloud connection to back-office warehouse and billing systems.

---

## 2. Target Persona & Use Cases
- **FMCG / Food & Beverage Distributors:** Fast van sales and secondary order booking.
- **Pharmaceutical Sales Reps:** Chemist visits, sample distribution, and order tracking.
- **Building Materials / Hardware Distribution:** Bulky SKU ordering with custom dealer pricing.
