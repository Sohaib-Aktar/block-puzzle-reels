## 1. Marketing Strategy & Audience Insight

### 1.1 Emotional Hook & Value Proposition
- **The Core Problem:** Modern workdays are mentally draining. The evening commute is crowded and noisy. By the time someone reaches home, they want a low-stress, relaxing mental reset that doesn't demand intense energy, competitive pressure, or active internet.
- **The Solution:** Block Puzzle Burst is the perfect 5-minute personal escape. Relaxing sounds, smooth tactile block placements, zero timers, zero stress.
- **The Story Arc:**
  $$\text{Tired at Office (0–3s)} \longrightarrow \text{Evening Commute (3–6s)} \longrightarrow \text{Exhausted on Sofa (6–9s)} \longrightarrow \text{Phone Pick-up (9–12s)} \longrightarrow \text{Game Open (12–15s)} \longrightarrow \text{Satisfying Gameplay (15–20s)} \longrightarrow \text{Pyapiras Brand Outro (20–22s)}$$

---

## 2. Character & Visual Continuity Specifications

To ensure the lifestyle sequence feels like **one person's real journey**, strict continuity rules apply:
- **Character Profile:** Young professional (24–35 years old), neat smart-casual attire.
- **Costume:** Dark navy crew-neck sweater or charcoal blazer over white shirt, dark trousers, wristwatch on left wrist.
- **Physical Traits:** Consistent face, haircut, and skin tone across all 4 lifestyle scenes.
- **Environment Lighting:**
  - Scene 1: Office fluorescent / laptop screen glow.
  - Scene 2: Evening transit window reflections, amber streetlights.
  - Scene 3: Warm living room lamplight.
  - Scene 4: Soft indoor light with smartphone screen illumination.
