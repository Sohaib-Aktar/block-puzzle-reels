# Social Media Marketing Strategy & Creative Guidelines
**AI Social Media Content Studio — Standard Operating Marketing Playbook**

---

## 1. Core Marketing Principles

### A. Audience-Centric Angle Before Rendering
Never produce a video simply because video tools exist. Every single video must serve a verified stage of the viewer's journey:
1. **Cold Attention (Discovery):** Stop the scroll within 1.5–2.0 seconds with high contrast, curiosity, or an acute relatable frustration.
2. **Engagement & Retention:** Deliver immediate payoff or cognitive challenge without filler.
3. **Conversion / Community:** Prompt exactly one specific micro-action (Comment, Save, Bio Link, Subscribe).

### B. Anti-Repetition & Hook Fatigue System
To prevent audience boredom and algorithm penalties:
- Never repeat the same opening hook phrasing across consecutive posts.
- Rotate through 5 hook archetypes:
  - **Curiosity / Challenge:** *"Solve it in ONE move!"*
  - **Relatable Frustration:** *"Tired of Excel payroll breaking every month?"*
  - **Speed / Satisfaction:** *"Watch 10 retail items scanned in 12 seconds."*
  - **Story / Transparency:** *"I built an offline game alone in West Bengal."*
  - **Educational / Counter-Intuitive:** *"Why 90% of warehouse pick errors happen at the bin."*

### C. The Two-Part Engagement Loop (Gamification)
For games and puzzle challenges:
- **Part 1 (The Hook & Challenge):** Present an unresolved scenario (A/B/C). Ask the audience to vote/comment. Do NOT reveal the solution.
- **Part 2 (The Solution & Reveal):** Reveal the correct answer 24 hours later in Stories or a follow-up reel, congratulating those who got it right and inviting them to play the game via the bio link.

## 2. Platform Adaptation Rules

| Platform | Primary Job | Technical Specs | Caption Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | Viral discovery & comments | 1080×1920 (9:16), ≤30s sweet spot | Conversational hook, question prompt, 3–5 niche hashtags |
| **Facebook Reels** | Broad demographic reach | 1080×1920 (9:16), ≤60s | Direct value statement, relatable tone, clear direct link |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Algorithmic subscriber growth | 1080×1920 (9:16), ≤60s | High keyword density in title, `#Shorts`, pinned comment link |
| **YouTube Longform** | High-intent tutorial / demo | 1920×1080 (16:9), 5–15 min | Timestamp chapters, actionable tutorial, problem-to-solution |

---

## 3. Language & Localisation Standards
- **On-Screen Text:** English only by default. High contrast typography, bold weights, central safe area placement.
- **Social Post Copy:** Multi-lingual caption structure (English primary, followed by Bengali and Hindi for regional resonance).
- **Audio:** Voiceover or high-energy background music that does not drown out sound effects.

---

## 4. Single-Action CTA Rotation
Never ask for two things at once (e.g. do not say "Like, comment, share and click the link"). Pick ONE:
- Day A: "Which piece goes first? Comment A, B or C!" (Maximizes comment velocity)
- Day B: "Save this for your next commute 📌" (Maximizes algorithm save signals)
- Day C: "Ready to test your brain? Free link in bio 🔗" (Drives installs)
